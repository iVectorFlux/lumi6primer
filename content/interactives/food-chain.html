Food Chain — Ecosystem Experiment

Change the number of grasshoppers. Watch the food chain react over time.

The ecosystem is in balance Plants provide food for grasshoppers. Grasshoppers provide food for frogs. Frogs provide food for snakes.

Try it Drag grasshoppers almost all the way down. Don't just look at the grasshoppers — wait and watch what happens to the plants, frogs, and then snakes. The key idea: organisms depend on other organisms for food.
